Big Two

Four players, thirteen cards each. Empty your hand before anyone else does.

DifficultyHow cleverly the bots play

How to play

Be the first to play all 13 of your cards. Each turn you either play a hand that beats the last one, or pass.

Card order

Ranks run 3 (lowest) up to A, then 2 as the highest card. Suits break ties:

Hands you can play

  • A single card, a pair, or three of a kind (all the same rank).
  • Any five-card hand, listed here from weakest to strongest:

Beating a play

  • Match the same kind of hand: a pair beats a pair, a single beats a single.
  • Five-card hands beat any weaker five-card hand, or a higher one of the same type.
  • Straights run 3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A, then A-2-3-4-5 and 2-3-4-5-6 as the two highest. J-Q-K-A-2 does not count.
  • Flushes compare their highest card. Full houses compare the triple. Four of a kind takes any fifth card.

Turns and passing

  • Whoever holds the 3 of Diamonds leads, and the first play must include it.
  • After a pass you sit out until the round ends. When all three others pass, the last player to play leads anything.

Scoring

When someone goes out, every other player pays them one point per card left. With 8 or 9 left it doubles, 10 to 12 triples, and a full 13 quadruples.
